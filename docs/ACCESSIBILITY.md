# Accessibility acceptance

Native controls have descriptive labels, semantic headings, selected/disabled states and minimum 48-point touch targets. Text scales with the system font setting. The full-screen navigation exposes a close action and Android Back dismissal, isolates the underlying content from accessibility focus, and scrolls when large text needs additional space.

The Home header, menu, buttons and safe areas are adapted from the BetweenBands mobile approach. Brand art/copy and blue/navy/gold colors come from BandCampNation, rather than the BetweenBands catalog.

Automated Android UI-tree checks verify primary destination labels and navigation. Before acceptance on both platforms, a human must test VoiceOver/TalkBack reading order, modal focus entry/return, 200% font scale, small/large screens, contrast, reduced motion and hardware-back behavior. Automated labels/screenshots do not prove screen-reader usability.
